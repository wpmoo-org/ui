# Code of Conduct

Moo UI should be a useful, respectful place for maintainers, contributors, and
users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections.
- Keep feedback specific to the work.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce an issue or understand a change.
- Respect different experience levels, tools, languages, and accessibility
  needs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.
- Dismissing accessibility, security, or compatibility concerns without
  evidence.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the private contact method
listed on the WPMoo organization profile or repository security policy. Public
issues are not appropriate for private conduct reports.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, block participants, or take
other moderation action needed to keep the project safe and focused.
